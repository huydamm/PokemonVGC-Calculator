VGC Live Calc privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-22. Applies to the VGC Live Calc browser extension and this website.

What the extension reads

On play.pokemonshowdown.com, the extension reads the battle you are watching or playing (the Pokémon on the field, their HP, boosts, field conditions, and your own team's stats) to compute damage numbers. This happens in your browser. Battle data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Network requests

To guess an opponent's likely set, the extension downloads public usage statistics from data.pkmn.cc and championsbattledata.com. These requests carry only the format and Pokémon being looked up, never your battle, account, or identity.

Payments

Purchases and free trials are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They store your email address and payment status so your purchase works across devices. We receive your email and whether you have paid, never your card details. See the ExtensionPay privacy policy.

What we do not do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data. The website stores nothing about you.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/huydamm/PokemonVGC-Calculator.
