VGC Live Calc privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-24. Applies to the VGC Live Calc browser extension and this website.

What the extension reads

On play.pokemonshowdown.com, the extension reads the battle you are watching or playing (the Pokémon on the field, their HP, boosts, field conditions, and your own team's stats) to compute damage numbers. This happens in your browser. Battle data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Network requests

To guess an opponent's likely set, the extension downloads public usage statistics from data.pkmn.cc and championsbattledata.com. These requests carry only the format and Pokémon being looked up, never your battle, account, or identity.

What the website loads

The calculator at vgccalc.app runs entirely in your browser. Your pasted team is never uploaded. To show sprites and suggest opponent sets, your browser requests images from play.pokemonshowdown.com and usage statistics from data.pkmn.cc (served by pkmn.github.io) and championsbattledata.com. Like any web request, these services and our host, GitHub Pages, can see your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. We receive none of it.

What we do not do

The extension is free, has no accounts, and stores nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data. The website sets no cookies and stores nothing about you, in your browser or anywhere else.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/huydamm/PokemonVGC-Calculator.
